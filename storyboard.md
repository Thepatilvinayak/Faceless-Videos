# Storyboard — Lakshmana and the sleepless vow

## Design lock
Original Tholu Bommalata-inspired, procedural coloured leather-cutout look: amber moonlight, maroon and turmeric-gold garments, pierced leather dots, dark ink contour, visible hinge discs. Portrait 9:16; forest-night theatre. Lakshmana is shown as a dignified archer with a simple crown and bow. The drifting gold rings are a symbolic visual question about sleep, not an added spoken answer. No extra dialogue.

## Motion plan (30 fps; one 7.2 s shot with changing staged beats)
- 00:00–00:02.05 — Establish Lakshmana on a forest night beneath moon; slight body bob and ambient motes. Caption: “FOR FOURTEEN YEARS”.
- 00:02.05–00:03.65 — A visible elbow-hinged forearm rises in an eased gesture; torso breathes subtly; caption: “LAKSHMANA NEVER SLEPT.”
- 00:03.65–00:05.63 — Arm settles and a golden sleep motif drifts from the hand; caption: “SO WHO CARRIED HIS SLEEP?”
- 00:05.63–00:07.20 — Motif continues to float; narration has landed, hold a short visual tail.

## Rig and animation
The character is drawn in code as separated torso/head/legs/bow and articulated upper arm/forearm elements. The forearm rotates about an elbow rivet with a keyed eased angle (roughly -72 degrees), not a whole-image pan/zoom. A body bob, drifting motif and ambient stage layers supply independent motion. Frames are generated at 540x960 then encoded at 1080x1920.

## Assumptions
No audio file was supplied, and the linked Shorts recording could not be retrieved from the rendering workspace. User selected generated narration instead. Narration therefore uses the exact requested words and a newly selected synthetic voice; the linked performance is not represented as preserved. The visual treatment is an interpretive question, not a claim to quote an unprovided source.
